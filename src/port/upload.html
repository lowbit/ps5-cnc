Send Red Alert to PS5 Native RA

The console reads the game files out of whatever you have, so send it as it is:

  • Disc images (.iso, .bin, .img, .mdf) or archives (.zip, .7z, .rar) of the Allied, Soviet, Counterstrike or Aftermath discs, such as EA's free RedAlert1_AlliedDisc.rar and RedAlert1_SovietDisc.rar.
  • The folder of an installed copy: The Ultimate Collection (EA app or Steam), the Remastered Collection (its Data folder), or Red Alert installed from its discs or from The First Decade. The First Decade's DVD works too, as its image or its files.

From a folder only the files the game needs are sent (REDALERT.MIX, MAIN.MIX, EXPAND2.MIX and the like, disc images and archives), however large the folder is.

The console is not listening. On the PS5, open PS5 Native RA and choose Send files from a PC or phone.
