Put the Red Alert game files here. File names may be in any case.

  redalert.mix            from INSTALL/REDALERT.MIX on either Red Alert disc
  allied/main.mix         MAIN.MIX from the Allied disc
  soviet/main.mix         MAIN.MIX from the Soviet disc
  counterstrike/main.mix  MAIN.MIX from the Counterstrike disc (optional)
  aftermath/main.mix      MAIN.MIX from the Aftermath disc (optional)
  expand.mix              Counterstrike's missions (optional)
  expand2.mix, hires1.mix, lores1.mix   Aftermath's units and missions (optional)

One disc is enough to start. The 2008 freeware Allied and Soviet disc images
hold everything the base game has: both campaigns, the movies and the music.
The official demo works too: its INSTALL/REDALERT.MIX and INSTALL/MAIN.MIX
both go straight in this folder.

Settings and saves are kept in the title's save data, not here.
